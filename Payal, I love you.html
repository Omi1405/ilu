Payal, I love you. Bahut zyada.

Mujhe tumhari hansi bahut pasand hai, jo poore kamre ko halka aur khushnuma bana deti hai. Mujhe pasand hai ki tum apne logon ki kitni parwah karti ho, aur main khushnaseeb hoon ki main bhi unmein se ek hoon.

Tumhare saath normal din bhi yaadgaar ban jaate hain. Ek chhoti si walk, koi pagal joke, ya shaant si shaam, sab mere din ka sabse pyaara hissa ban jaata hai.

Tum mujhe better, softer aur braver banne ki wajah deti ho. Tumne mujhe ek insaan mein ghar de diya hai, aur main wahan se kabhi jaana nahi chahta.

Main ye aksar kehta nahi, isliye aaj saaf-saaf keh raha hoon: tum meri zindagi ki sabse achhi cheez ho.

Tumse pyaar karne ki wajah

Button dabao, main ek wajah bataunga.

Main tumse jitna keh sakta hoon, usse kahin zyada pyaar karta hoon, Payal.
